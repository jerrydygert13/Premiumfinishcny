FAQ

Good questions

Don't see yours? Call or text 315-641-9988 — we're happy to help.

Where do you do the detailing?
Wherever your car is. We're fully mobile — we come to your house, your work, or your apartment, so there's no drop-off and no waiting around.
Is the listed price final?
Final pricing is determined by vehicle condition. Package selection is based on the level of care required to deliver professional results — entry-level packages apply only to vehicles in suitable condition. SUVs and trucks are +$30 on packages and +$15/month on maintenance plans.
Which package is right for me?
Express Detail is a quick, detailed clean inside and out. Executive Detail adds a deeper clean and protection — light shampoo, stain and pet hair removal, steam, leather conditioning and wax sealant. Premium Finish is our full restoration: heavy stains, full pet hair removal, deep shampoo, clay bar and UV protection.
How do maintenance plans work?
Pick Basic ($89/month) or Premium ($119/month) and we'll keep your vehicle looking its best on a regular schedule. Plans are monthly and you can cancel anytime.
How do I book?
Use our booking page — pick your service and any add-ons, then send us your details. We'll reach out to confirm a time. You can also call or text us any time at 315-641-9988.
Can I add extra services?
Yes. Engine Bay Cleaning, Trim Restoration, Clay Bar & Paint Decon, Headlight Restoration and Protective Wax Sealant can be added to any package when you book.
